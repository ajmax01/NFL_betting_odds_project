# Predictive Accuracy of NFL Betting Odds

This project involved running regressions of NFL game outcome prediction accuracy on team market sizes. Match outcomes were utilized from multiple recent NFL seasons and Nielsen DMA reporting provided market size data. The models strongly showed that market sizes have no effect on the accuracy of betting odds, i.e. betting odds are equally accurate regardless of whether a team is in a large or small market. At that, betting odds were found to be highly accurate in general.
Additional extensions were examined, for example extreme weather scenarios, indoor-vs-outdoor stadiums, and playoff situations.
